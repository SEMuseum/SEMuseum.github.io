WAYS TO COLLABORATE

合作方式Ways to Collaborate

与机构、媒体、研究者共建社会企业知识生态Building the knowledge ecosystem with institutions, media and researchers

🗂️

数据共建Data Co-building

共享机构、项目与文献资料，共同扩充开放数据库，让更多知识被检索与复用。Share organization, project and document resources to expand the open database.

🔬

研究合作Research

为高校与研究者提供结构化数据支持，联合开展社会企业领域议题研究。Providing structured data support and joint research on social enterprise topics.

📣

媒体传播Media

与媒体和自媒体联动，传播社会企业理念与可信资讯，放大正向影响。Partnering with media to spread social enterprise ideas and trustworthy information.

🎪

活动联办Events

联合举办论坛、工作坊与线上分享，连接实践者与关注者。Co-hosting forums, workshops and online talks connecting practitioners and supporters.

HOW WE COOPERATE

合作怎么开始How it Starts

01

发起联系Reach Out

通过微信或邮箱说明合作意向与初步想法。Share your interest and initial ideas via WeChat or email.

02

沟通对齐Align

明确合作目标、分工与时间安排，形成共识。Clarify goals, roles and timeline to reach alignment.

03

协作推进Execute

志愿者团队与合作伙伴协同推进，定期同步进展。Volunteers and partners work together with regular updates.

04

成果共享Share

以公益方式开放合作成果，让价值惠及更多人。Openly share outcomes for the public good.
