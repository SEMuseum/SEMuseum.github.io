WHO WE ARE

由志愿者共建的开放知识项目An Open Knowledge Project Built by Volunteers

社企博物是一个由志愿者组成的去中心化公益知识项目。我们以收集、整理、共享、传播社会企业及可持续发展相关资料为目的，把分散在各处的公开信息汇聚成可检索、可复用的公共知识资产。SEMuseum is a decentralized public-interest knowledge project run by volunteers. We collect, sort, share and spread social enterprise and sustainability resources, turning scattered public information into a searchable, reusable public asset.

在信息日益过载的时代，我们希望通过结构化梳理，让关注社会企业与可持续领域的每一个人在更短时间内获取可信信息——从文献到影像，从人物到机构，从中国到世界。In an age of information overload, we use structured curation to help everyone interested in social enterprise and sustainability access trustworthy information faster — from documents to images, people to organizations, China to the world.

我们不做信息的搬运工，而是做知识的整理者：以公益之心，共建开放共享的社会企业知识基础设施。We are not merely information carriers, but knowledge curators — building an open knowledge infrastructure for social enterprise with a public spirit.

HOW WE WORK

我们的运作模式How We Work

01

去中心化协作Decentralized

志愿者分布式协作，各展所长，按兴趣与时间参与。Volunteers collaborate distributedly, contributing by strength, interest and available time.

02

开放数据Open Data

以公益方式开放数据，让知识成为人人可用的公共资产。Opening data for public good, making knowledge a public asset for all.

03

结构化治理Structured

以多维表格与字典表标准化管理，保障数据的可检索与可扩展。Standardized management via structured tables and dictionaries for searchability and scalability.

04

持续更新Continuous

资讯与数据定期更新，让知识库始终保持时效与活力。Regularly updating news and data to keep the knowledge base current and alive.
